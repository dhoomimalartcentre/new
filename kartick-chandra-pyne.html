Kartick
Chandra Pyne

Painter • born 1931

One of the three painters credited with bringing surrealism into modern Indian art.

Surrealism
in Calcutta

Kartick Chandra Pyne was born in 1931 into a family of goldsmiths. Drawn to painting from childhood, he made it his life's work, taking a diploma in fine arts from the Government College of Art and Craft, Calcutta, in 1955, and later a scholarship from the Academy of Fine Arts, Calcutta (1967–69).

In 100 Years of Modern Indian Art, Dr. Laxmi P. Sihare names him as one of the three painters who introduced surrealism to Indian art.

He showed regularly at the Academy of Fine Arts' annual exhibitions in Calcutta and at the Lalit Kala Akademi, New Delhi, and Jehangir Art Gallery, Bombay (both 1968). His solo exhibitions include New Delhi (1961) and the Academy of Fine Arts, Calcutta (1967, 1980, 1982), and in 1979 his work was part of the Asian Artists exhibition at the Fukuoka Art Museum in Japan. In 2022 Dhoomimal presented Shades and Shine of Kartick Chandra Pyne, together with a book of the same name.
