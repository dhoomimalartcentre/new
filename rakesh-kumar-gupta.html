Rakesh
Kumar Gupta

Painter & sculptor • Delhi

Crisp, bracing heads and figures in robust blue line, each one meant to stir a feeling in the viewer.

Heads, figures
and feeling

“Art as a language of communication is instinct with the need to express, to evoke or provoke feelings.”

Born in Delhi in 1960, Rakesh Kumar Gupta holds an MA in Drawing and Painting. His work is crisp and bracing, with robust lines in blue that move towards sculptural form. Through a range of heads and figures, each with its own expressive meaning, he uses oblique references, tone, colour and imagery to create vivid sensations for the viewer.

He has shown in state, national and international exhibitions since 1986, including the Triennale India in 1994. Dhoomimal Art Centre held his solo exhibitions in 1994, 1997 and 2009, and he has had solo shows at Art Indus, Delhi (1999) and in Hong Kong (2003). His group shows include Dhoomimal's The Millennium Mask (2000), Kites (2003) and Portrait of an Artist (2011), as well as the India Art Fair (2015) and the Affordable Art Fair in Singapore (2016).

His work is held by the National Gallery of Modern Art, New Delhi, the Glenbarra Art Museum in Japan and the Sahitya Kala Parishad, and he has given work in support of Art for Odisha, the Gujarat Relief Fund and People for Animals.
