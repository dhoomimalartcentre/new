Pradiptaa
Chakraborty

Painter • West Bengal

Colourful, semi-realist paintings that read life as a juggler's game, full of hidden desires and fleeting images.

A chronicle
of humanism

For Pradiptaa Chakraborty, desire is the hidden motive of the living soul, and every eye is caught in the dilemma of satisfaction. His paintings, in strong colour and a semi-realist manner, reflect the many images of life: a kaleidoscope of fleeting moments, joys and disappointments. Life, in his work, is a juggler's game, where the conjurer on the road plays out his tricks to entertain the crowd and to veil the hidden instincts of human life.

Born in West Bengal in 1979, he took a BFA in Graphics from Kala Bhavana, Visva-Bharati, Santiniketan (1998–2003), where he held a merit scholarship, and a five-year diploma in painting from Rabindra Bharati University, Kolkata. In 2003–04 he received the Government of India's National Scholarship for Young Artists.

He has exhibited since 2002, in India and in London, Sydney, Japan, Dubai and Singapore. His solo shows include Passion Play at Sarjan Art Gallery, Baroda (2008), Suladharini at Sensorium, Kolkata (2014, 2015), and a show at Arushi Art Gallery, Delhi (2016), and he has shown at the India Art Fair (2017, 2018). His paintings are in collections in India and abroad, including Cedars-Sinai in Los Angeles.
