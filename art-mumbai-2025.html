Art Mumbai 2025

Nov 2025 • Mumbai, India
art fairbooth A13modern & contemporary
  • Dates13 – 16 November 2025
  • Hours11 am – 8 pm
  • BoothA13
  • VenueMahalaxmi Racecourse, Mumbai

In November 2025 Dhoomimal Art Centre took part in Art Mumbai, bringing original works by modern and contemporary Indian artists to booth A13 at the Mahalaxmi Racecourse for four days.

At the fair

The centre's booth was open to visitors from 13 to 16 November, from 11 am to 8 pm each day, alongside the other galleries at the fair.

Original works only

As at the gallery in Connaught Place, every work on the stand was an original. Dhoomimal does not deal in reproductions of any kind.

From Connaught Place to Mumbai

Art fairs take the gallery beyond its home at A-8, Connaught Place. Dhoomimal showed at the India Art Fair in New Delhi from 2013 to 2016, and Art Mumbai brought its artists to collectors in Mumbai.

Enquiries

For works shown at the fair, call +91 11 4151 6172 or write to info@dhoomimalartcentre.com, or visit the contact page.
