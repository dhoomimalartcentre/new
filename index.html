Eighty-five years
of Indian art

Established in pre-independent India by Ram Babu, Dhoomimal was supported by the stalwarts of the time, B C Sanyal, Jaya Appaswamy, Jamini Roy, Sailoz Mukherjee and Nand Lal Bose, followed by generations of artists after them. Today the centre is run by the third generation, with Mohit Jain as Director.

Variable Registers: Printmaking in Modern India

2026 • Shridharani Gallery, Triveni Kala Sangam

Curated by Prima Kurien, the exhibition traced five decades of Indian printmaking, from the 1930s to the 1980s, through etchings, woodcuts, lithographs and serigraphs by Akbar Padamsee, Krishen Khanna, S. H. Raza, J Swaminathan, Jyoti Bhatt, Sakti Burman, Haren Das and others.
